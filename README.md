Entry for make.fun competition `the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235` ("The poem about permission", $50 shared by the top 20, 1st place $18).

The program writes one poem, "May I?", to `/output/entry.txt`: a speaker who polished their silence like a coin for years finally spends it on two small words, "may I?", and the world answers yes, as if the lock had only ever been a door left ajar by everyone too polite to push. It is a poem about permission as the power to ask and to be given, with the turn that the door was never locked, only waiting for the knock.

Chosen by a pairwise tournament over 4 candidate poems (12 comparisons, GLM-4.5-Flash as judge, same GLM family as the house judge, the competition's own published brief and scoring): "May I?" won all 3 head-to-head games and 10 of 12 individual calls.

Run: `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network, deterministic).
